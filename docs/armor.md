# Armor

Four tiers. All pieces are fire resistant and every piece has a passive ability.

## Overview

| Tier | Helmet / Chest / Legs / Boots | Total | Toughness (set) | Knockback res. (set) | Enchantability | Durability | How to get |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](assets/icons/solarite_chestplate.png){ .mc-inline } Solarite | 4 / 7 / 9 / 4 | 24 | 14 | 60% | 85 | 12,000 | Crafting table (ingots) |
| ![](assets/icons/emberite_chestplate.png){ .mc-inline } Emberite | 5 / 8 / 10 / 5 | 28 | 18 | 80% | 105 | 24,000 | Crafting: Solarite piece + Emberite Ingots |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_chestplate.png){ .mc-inline } Titanium | 6 / 9 / 9 / 6 | 30 | 20 | 100% | 125 | **Unbreakable** | Crafting: Emberite piece + Titanium Ingots |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_star_chestplate.png){ .mc-inline } Titanium Star | 3000 each\* | 30\* | 20 | 100% | 145 | **Unbreakable** | Smithing: Titanium piece + Titanium Star |

\* Minecraft caps total armor at 30 and toughness at 20. The Titanium Star shows 3000 per piece, but its real
strength is the [full set bonus](#titanium-star-extras).

For comparison: a full netherite set gives 20 armor, 12 toughness and 40% knockback resistance.

## Abilities (every tier)

| Piece | Ability |
|---|---|
| ![](assets/icons/solarite_helmet.png){ .mc-inline } Helmet | **Aqua Affinity**: normal mining speed underwater |
| ![](assets/icons/solarite_chestplate.png){ .mc-inline } Chestplate | **Wither immunity** |
| ![](assets/icons/solarite_leggings.png){ .mc-inline } Leggings | **Fire & lava immunity** |
| ![](assets/icons/solarite_boots.png){ .mc-inline } Boots | **No fall damage** |

## Titanium Star extras

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Full set | **90% less damage** after armor and enchantments (does not apply to `/kill` or the void) |
| Helmet | **Infinite breath** and **Night Vision**, toggled with the **N** key (configurable) |
| Chestplate | **Creative flight** in survival (double-tap jump) |

A zombie hit deals about 0.09 damage to a full Titanium Star set; only absurd bosses can still hurt you.

## Armor bar

With a full set, the armor bar above the hotbar uses the material's own icon. If the set is incomplete, it shows the
icon of the tier below (Titanium Star → Titanium → Emberite → Solarite → vanilla).
