**TitanOres** is the endgame progression mod of the **Titan Modpack**. It adds a chain of rare ores spread across
the three dimensions, and a full line of tools, armor and special items that go well beyond netherite.

## Goals

- **A real endgame.** Every tier is clearly stronger than the last one, ending with Titanium Star gear.
- **Explore every dimension.** Each ore lives in one specific biome: deep oceans, the Warped Forest and the End Highlands.
- **Mine it yourself.** The ores cannot be mined by quarries/digital miners, are immune to explosions and only drop with the right pickaxe.
- **Modpack friendly.** Forge tags for every material, optional Curios support and configurable data (recipes, loot, tags) through datapacks.

## Installation (players)

1. Install **Forge 1.16.5-36.2.42** (or newer 36.x).
2. Drop `titanores-1.0.0.jar` into the instance `mods` folder.
3. Optional: add Curios API to wear the Solarite Magnet in a charm slot.

!!! tip "Look up recipes in game"
    Installing **JEI** (Just Enough Items) shows every TitanOres recipe, including smithing and armor upgrades.
