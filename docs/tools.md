# Tools

Four tiers. Each one is obtained from the previous tier, and **enchantments are kept** on every upgrade.

## Overview

| Tier | Harvest level | Durability | Mining speed | Enchantability | How to get |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](assets/icons/solarite_pickaxe.png){ .mc-inline } Solarite | 5 | 12,000 | 10 | 85 | Crafting table (ingots + Solarite Stick) |
| ![](assets/icons/emberite_pickaxe.png){ .mc-inline } Emberite | 6 | 24,000 | 15 | 85 | Smithing: Solarite tool + Emberite Ingot |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_pickaxe.png){ .mc-inline } Titanium | 7 | **Unbreakable** | 18 | 85 | Smithing: Emberite tool + Titanium Ingot |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_star_pickaxe.png){ .mc-inline } Titanium Star | 7 | **Unbreakable** | 26 | 85 | Smithing: Titanium tool + Titanium Star |

For comparison: netherite has level 4, 2,031 durability and mining speed 9.

## Attack damage

| Tier | Sword | Axe | Pickaxe | Shovel | Hoe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solarite | **25** (1.6/s) | 27 (1.0/s) | 7 | 7.5 | 1 |
| Emberite | **35** (1.2/s) | 37 | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| Titanium | **45** (1.0/s) | 47 | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Titanium Star | **220** (1.0/s) | 47 | 45 | 45 | 45 |

## Titanium abilities

Titanium and Titanium Star tools share the same abilities.

| Tool | Ability |
|---|---|
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_sword.png){ .mc-inline } Sword | **Built-in Fire Aspect**: sets targets on fire for 8 s. Mobs killed in one hit drop cooked food. |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_pickaxe.png){ .mc-inline } Pickaxe | **Area Mode**: mines 3×3 and **auto-smelts ores** (iron, gold, modded ores…). Silk Touch disables the smelting. |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_shovel.png){ .mc-inline } Shovel | **Area Mode**: digs 3×3 and auto-smelts ores. |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_hoe.png){ .mc-inline } Hoe | **Area Mode**: tills 3×3. |
| ![](assets/icons/titanium_axe.png){ .mc-inline } Axe | **Tree felling**: breaking any log fells the whole tree (up to 256 logs). |

!!! tip "Area Mode"
    - **Shift + right-click** toggles Area Mode (message `Area Mode: ON/OFF` above the hotbar). It starts **ON**.
    - Sneaking while mining breaks a single block.
    - The 3×3 never breaks bedrock or blocks much harder than the one you mine (no obsidian when digging stone).

!!! tip "Tree felling safety"
    The axe only fells **natural trees** (logs touching natural leaves or nether wart blocks), so log houses are safe.
    Sneak to break a single log.

!!! note "Unbreakable tools"
    Titanium tools never lose durability, so Unbreaking and Mending are not offered for them. Every other
    enchantment works, including the enchanting table.
